Most teams treat design as the last mile of execution. I treat it as the place a strategy first *meets reality* — which is exactly where the leverage is.

## 01 / Frame the problem

The brief is rarely the real problem. I find the tension underneath it before anyone designs a screen.

## 02 / Make it concrete

Strategy stays an idea until it's a system someone can see, argue with, and actually build on.

## 03 / Make it shippable

I optimize for the handoff — documented, low-maintenance, built to hold its shape after I leave the room.
