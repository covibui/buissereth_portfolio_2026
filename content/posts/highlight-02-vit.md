## Problem

The founder behind VMC is opening a second practice — and this time wants the brand, the operating model, and the product experience settled before the doors open, not patched together after growth outran them the first time.

That's a different problem from VMC's. There's no legacy system to untangle, but there's also no forgiving grace period: whatever gets decided now is what the team will inherit on day one.

## Approach

I'm working brand, product vision, and operations in parallel rather than in sequence, so none of the three gets bolted onto a decision the others already made. The brand direction, the intake and booking experience, and the day-to-day workflows are being framed together, against the same set of constraints the first practice learned the hard way.

## Outcome

Still underway. The direction is set; the near-term work is turning it into the same kind of low-maintenance system VMC now runs on — just built in before launch this time, instead of retrofitted after.
