Most teams treat design as the last mile of execution. I treat it as the place a strategy first *meets reality* — which is exactly where the leverage is.

## 01 / Frame the problem

Before a screen, I find the real question. Most briefs describe a symptom; the work starts by naming what is actually broken.

## 02 / Make it concrete

Strategy that stays abstract dies in a deck. I turn it into systems, artifacts, and decisions a team can act on tomorrow.

## 03 / Make it shippable

A framed idea isn't finished until it runs without me. I build for handoff — documented, repeatable, low-maintenance.
