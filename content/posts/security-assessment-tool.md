## Problem

The security team's assessment backlog had outgrown the spreadsheet-and-instinct process used to prioritize it. Applications waited for review in roughly the order someone remembered they existed, not in order of actual risk — and there was no shared, legible view of where exposure was concentrated.

## Approach

I designed a self-serve risk dashboard that surfaced which application needed assessment next and why, turning scattered risk signals into one prioritized, visual queue. The emphasis was on data visualization the team could trust at a glance, not another report that needed a specialist to interpret.

## Outcome

A clear, prioritized view replaced assessment-by-instinct, giving the team a shared answer to "what's next" instead of a debate.
