## Problem

VMC's brand and operations had grown faster than its systems. Visual assets were
inconsistent across print and digital, and internal workflows lived in people's
heads — making it hard to scale or onboard staff.

## Approach

I audited the existing brand and operations, then built a single source of truth:
a design system covering digital and print assets, paired with documented,
repeatable workflows for the team. Every decision optimized for consistency and
low maintenance, so the clinic could run it without a designer on call.

## Outcome

A unified brand system and a set of operational templates the team uses daily.
Asset production time dropped, brand consistency went up, and onboarding new staff
became a process instead of a scramble.
