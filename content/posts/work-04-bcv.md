## Problem

Communication between the bank's branches and its customers had been shaped by what the existing systems could send, not by what a customer needed to know at that moment — so timely information and generic notices competed for the same inbox with no way to tell them apart.

## Approach

I built a forward-looking product vision for branch-to-customer communication, designed around the moments that mattered to a customer's decisions, and used it to argue for where the bank's systems needed to catch up to that reality.

## Outcome

A concrete vision the bank could evaluate its roadmap against — a reference point for closing the gap between what the systems send and what customers need to hear.
